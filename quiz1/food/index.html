Local Food in Tangerang

Laksa Tangerang

A traditional Tangerang noodle soup with a rich, savory broth, served with rice noodles, chicken, and flavorful spices.

Bolu Tape

A soft and sweet Indonesian sponge cake made with fermented cassava, giving it a distinctive sweet and slightly tangy flavor.

Sate Bandeng

A traditional Banten dish made from milkfish seasoned with spices, shaped back into the fish, and grilled until aromatic and flavorful.
